# Smart Office — Client Inputs and Dependencies

**Purpose:** Bid-ready statement of the decisions, information and participation needed from the client to price, plan, build and operate the proposed Smart Office solution. This is a request for client input, not a claim that the client has already supplied or approved it.

**Basis:** [Context Discovery](../context/smart-office-context.md) and the [approved High-Level Solution Design](../design/smart-office-hld.md). The initial scope described there is one Serbian office, about 200 employees, 150 bookable desks and 30 bookable parking spaces, with expansion to multiple offices and at least 5,000 users. The proposal covers the full solution; the Camp PoC is limited to 2–3 selected functionalities and may use synthetic data and simulated integrations.

## Proposed wording for the bid

> To deliver Smart Office successfully, we ask the client to nominate empowered business, workplace, IT, security and privacy contacts; confirm the operating policies and service targets below; and provide the relevant tenant, integration, environment and resource information at the agreed delivery milestones. We will validate these inputs in discovery and record the resulting decisions in the product backlog, architecture and acceptance criteria. The bid assumptions below allow a comparable proposal now; any material departure will be assessed jointly for impact on scope, cost, schedule and risk before implementation. The Camp PoC will use fictional or representative data and simulated integrations where permitted, so access to production systems or employee data is not required for that demonstration.

## 3. Proposed response and decision process

The client can respond to each **B** item with **decision**, **decision owner**, **date**, and any **policy document or constraint** that governs it. For each **D** item, the client and delivery team can agree a provider, format, environment and milestone. Unresolved items stay visible in a joint decision log; they should not silently become requirements or fixed-price commitments.

For bid comparison, the highest-priority responses are **B-01, B-02, B-05, B-08, B-09, B-11 and B-12** because they most directly affect scope, capacity, integration, compliance, service levels or the target platform. The remaining items can be confirmed in early discovery if the client accepts the stated provisional basis. If an input changes the agreed release boundary or service target, the delivery team should present the effect on design, cost and timeline for client decision.

## 4. Camp PoC dependency boundary

The selected PoC demonstrates booking conflict prevention, automatic release with simulated check-in evidence, and notification dispatch using mocked channels, as recorded in the [PoC selection](../poc/smart-office-poc-selection.md). It can proceed with fictional office/resource data and simulated identity, notification and check-in integrations under Context constraints C-01, C-03 and C-04. Client production tenant access, live badge/sensor feeds and actual employee data are delivery dependencies only if those integrations are included in an agreed production release.
